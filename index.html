Method

Prompt2Water sums the visible input context and the assistant output across all turns, adds a reasoning-token multiplier selected by the user, converts detected tools and media outputs into token-equivalent work using literature-based energy coefficients, and finally converts the resulting energy into direct and electricity-related water consumption.
