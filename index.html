How it works

For each assistant response, Prompt2Water counts the visible conversation context and generated text, applies a model-class energy coefficient, adds visible tool use, and converts the resulting energy into direct and electricity-related water consumption.

Echat = Σ(text energy + visible tool energy)
Wchat = 4.1 mL/Wh × Echat
Parameters and references
  • Output model classes: 0.15, 0.50, 1.00 and 1.50 Wh per 1000 generated tokens.
  • Input-token cost increases with visible context length.
  • Thinking levels add 0, 1, 3, 7 or 14 output-token equivalents.
  • Visible web, code, image and video operations are added separately.
  • Water conversion: 0.2 mL/Wh direct and 3.9 mL/Wh electricity-related.
